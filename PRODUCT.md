# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js 16 App Router with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, and native browser scrolling, chosen from the user's mandatory stack and performance requirements.

## Users

Primary users are people preparing for visa and immigration processes who need a premium, trustworthy first contact before sending documentation. A secondary audience is the company team that will later edit company data, contact information, and multilingual copy manually.

## Product Purpose

This website presents a premium immigration-document preparation company and helps visitors understand the offer, trust the process, and start their documentation workflow with more clarity and organization.

## Positioning

The product frames immigration document preparation as a precise, editorial, internationally minded service: organized, human, multilingual, and premium without presenting itself as a law firm or promising approvals.

## Operating Context

The main flow is a multilingual marketing website with localized routes, strong first-viewport storytelling, service explanation, process explanation, and a clear CTA to start contact. The experience must feel premium on desktop and mobile, use real photography, and remain easy to edit after delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three locales are required: Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
- Browser language should influence the first redirect, but a manual language choice must persist.
- Copy must remain centralized, editable, and separate from component logic.
- The company prepares and organizes documentation; it must not be presented as legal representation by default.
- Performance, accessibility, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion handling are hard requirements.
- This run uses placeholder company data and curated Unsplash imagery until the user replaces them.

## Brand Commitments

- The visual direction is premium, editorial, technical, and internationally oriented.
- The palette is anchored in black, white, and a restrained precision accent of `#FF3D00`.
- The site must not resemble a generic SaaS template, old-fashioned legal office, or animation showcase.

## Evidence on Hand

- Detailed implementation brief from the user covering structure, stack, art direction, motion, accessibility, and content rules.
- No confirmed company-specific assets, testimonials, or legal claims were provided in the repository at the start of this run.

## Product Principles

- Make complexity feel organized before the visitor ever sends a file.
- Use editorial composition and technical precision to earn trust quickly.
- Let motion guide hierarchy and continuity, never distract from reading.
- Keep the system easy to maintain by centralizing content, media, and company data.
- Treat multilingual clarity and accessibility as product quality, not polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, translated alt text, and reduced-motion behavior are required.
- The experience must remain legible and usable across desktop and mobile viewport classes.
